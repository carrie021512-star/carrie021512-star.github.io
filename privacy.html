Privacy policy

Personal-use scope

This policy describes a private Hermes Assistant integration operated by carrie021512-star. It is not a public service accepting other people’s Google accounts.

Data accessed and purposes

With the operator’s OAuth consent, the integration can access Gmail messages and metadata, Calendar events, and authorized Drive files, spreadsheets, and documents. It uses those data for personal email assistance, job-search tracking, meeting and reservation routing, and document or spreadsheet maintenance. Requested and granted permissions determine which operations are available.

Storage and processing

OAuth credentials are stored on the operator’s private machine. Task outputs, email excerpts, conversation history, and records may be retained in private local state or in the operator’s authorized Google files. Relevant task content can be transmitted to the operator’s configured AI providers and, for notifications or assistant conversations, connected messaging services. Those services process data under their own terms and privacy policies.

Google user data is not sold or used for advertising. The integration does not intentionally use Google Workspace user data to develop, improve, or train generalized AI or machine-learning models. Before processing Google data, the operator must choose provider settings and terms compatible with Google’s Limited Use requirements.

Control and deletion

The operator can revoke access in Google Account third-party connections, remove local OAuth credentials, disable automated tasks, and delete retained records from local storage and authorized Google files. Copies held by AI or messaging providers are governed by those providers’ retention and deletion controls. Revoking OAuth does not itself delete previously retained records.

This website

These informational pages do not request Google login, set application cookies, or contain analytics scripts. GitHub hosts the site and may record visitor information, including IP addresses, under the GitHub Privacy Statement. No Gmail data or OAuth secrets are published here.

Limited Use and contact

Hermes Assistant’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

For questions, contact the operator through the GitHub profile. This policy should be updated whenever the integration’s data practices change.
