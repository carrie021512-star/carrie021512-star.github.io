Hermes Assistant

Hermes Assistant is a private, personal-use integration connecting the operator’s authorized Google accounts to a self-hosted Hermes Agent.

Purpose and Google access

It reads authorized Gmail messages to track job applications and interview updates, and identify meeting invitations and reservations. It can manage Google Calendar events for the operator’s configured routing workflows. Where authorized, it reads or updates Google Drive, Sheets, and Docs used for personal records.

Access is limited to the accounts and permissions the operator authorizes. Email sending or mailbox changes require the operator’s instructions or approval. This website is informational only: it does not provide sign-in, collect Google credentials, or expose the private assistant.

Google data may be processed by the configured AI provider to carry out the operator’s tasks. See the privacy policy and terms of use.
